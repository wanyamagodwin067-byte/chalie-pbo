Youth mental health and wellbeing

You do not have to face it alone.

CHALIE PBO supports young people through mental-health education, safe peer-support spaces, community programmes and referrals to appropriate professional care in Eldoret, Kenya.

Important safety information

This website is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, needs urgent medical help, or may harm themselves or another person, go to the nearest hospital or contact local emergency services.
